# Presentation Notes

An earlier 20-slide project-journey presentation was created, but the feedback was that it was too technical.

## Revision direction

The revised presentation should be professional, clear, and natural. It should tell the story of the work:
- What I did.
- Why I did it.
- What I noticed.
- What I learned.
- What the results could mean for the college.

Keep technical explanations only where they help the audience understand the work. Include spaces for:
- Project overview and workflow screenshots.
- The selected business questions.
- The dashboard pages and verified findings.
- Challenges and limitations.
- Recommendations to the institution.
- Conclusion and next steps.

The user plans to provide a Canva template. Adapt the slide structure and visual style to that template when available.

Do not invent results. Use actual Power BI screenshots and validate the measures before stating findings as final.
